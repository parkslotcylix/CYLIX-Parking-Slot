# Custom Date Range - Quick Guide

**Status**: ✅ ACTIVE  
**Date**: May 7, 2026

---

## What's New

A custom date range filter option in Analytics that lets you select any date range for viewing parking statistics.

---

## How To Use

### Step 1: Open Analytics
1. Go to Analytics page
2. Look at the filter dropdown

### Step 2: Select Custom Range
1. Click the filter dropdown
2. Select "Custom Range"
3. Date input fields appear

### Step 3: Choose Dates
1. Click "Start Date" field
2. Select start date from calendar
3. Click "End Date" field
4. Select end date from calendar

### Step 4: Apply
1. Click "Apply" button
2. Analytics updates with your date range
3. All metrics recalculate

---

## Filter Options

| Option | Period |
|--------|--------|
| Today | Current day |
| Yesterday | Previous day |
| This Week | Last 7 days |
| 1 Month | Last 30 days |
| **Custom Range** | **Any dates you choose** |

---

## Example Usage

### View May 1-7 Data
1. Select "Custom Range"
2. Start Date: May 1, 2026
3. End Date: May 7, 2026
4. Click "Apply"
5. See analytics for May 1-7

### View Specific Week
1. Select "Custom Range"
2. Start Date: April 29, 2026
3. End Date: May 5, 2026
4. Click "Apply"
5. See analytics for that week

### View Specific Month
1. Select "Custom Range"
2. Start Date: May 1, 2026
3. End Date: May 31, 2026
4. Click "Apply"
5. See analytics for May

## What Updates

When you apply a custom date range:
- ✅ Total Sessions Completed
- ✅ Average Parking Duration
- ✅ Available Parking Slots
- ✅ Active Sessions
- ✅ Occupancy Rate Chart
- ✅ Peak Hours Chart
- ✅ Print Report

---

## Print Report

### With Custom Range
1. Select custom date range
2. Click "📄 Print Report"
3. Report shows your date range in title
4. All data is for your selected period
5. Click "Print" to print

### Report Title Example
```
🅿️ Parking Analytics Report
Period: May 1, 2026 to May 7, 2026
Generated: May 7, 2026 at 2:30 PM
```

---

## Validation

### What Gets Checked
- ✅ Both dates must be selected
- ✅ Start date must be before end date

### Error Messages
```
"Please select both start and end dates"
"Start date must be before end date"
```

---

## Default Values

When you select "Custom Range":
- **Start Date**: 30 days ago
- **End Date**: Today

You can change these to any dates you want.
